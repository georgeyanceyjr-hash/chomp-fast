Method changes from V3
----------------------
1. Boundary expansion children are routed directly to their known (width,height)
   shard, avoiding repeated popcount/bit-length class recovery.
2. Interior corner moves use a specialized bit transform. Because these moves
   retain the leading one and trailing zero, general normalization is a no-op.
3. Once a whole class is above the immutable seed frontier, corner probes use
   its already-known ClassShard directly.
4. The first computed class overlaps the seed frontier, so it deliberately uses
   the general seed-aware lookup. This fallback is required for correctness.
5. The production executable uses GCC profile-guided optimization trained on
   the representative R(17,13) workload, plus -O3 -march=native -flto.

Rejected experiment
-------------------
An earlier V4 attempt bypassed the seed-aware lookup even in the frontier-
overlapping class. Small and fully preloaded replay tests did not expose it;
fresh production did, beginning at R(18,11). That checkpoint and log are
rejected and are not part of the package. The corrected fallback above was then
validated at the first divergent class before the accepted production.
